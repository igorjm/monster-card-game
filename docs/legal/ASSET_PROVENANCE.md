# Asset provenance audit

The machine-readable inventory is `ASSET_PROVENANCE.json`. `review-required` means the file may be used for internal development but cannot enter a commercial build or store listing.

For each asset, replace placeholders with source files, author, creation date, tool/model and settings, performers, license, third-party samples, releases and reviewer. The retained narration additionally needs the original recording session and performer consent. Fonts loaded from Google and current icons require the same distribution review.

`public/audio/rj/` is deliberately untracked and was not modified. It must not be copied into a build until separately inventoried and approved.
