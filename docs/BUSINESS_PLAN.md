# Business plan — Brazil-first

## Positioning

Private, invitation-only social deduction for families and friends aged 13+, operated by a verified adult host. The differentiation is not the underlying rules: it is frictionless guest access, family voice/video controls, original worlds, deterministic host progression, accessibility, rematches and one purchase shared by the whole room.

## Offers

| Offer | Price | Account behavior |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Vila das Criaturas | Free | Starter; one internal post-match promotion to eligible adult hosts |
| Remove ads | R$ 14,90 | Permanent suppression in every starter room hosted by the account |
| Premium world | R$ 12,90 | Permanent host entitlement; all guests use it without ads |
| Founders bundle | R$ 34,90 | Remove ads plus the first three premium worlds |

No subscriptions, consumables, randomized rewards or gameplay power. XP, achievements, titles, frames, backgrounds, role mastery and rematch milestones are deterministic and cosmetic.

## Launch worlds

- Starter: `Vila das Criaturas`, a generic creature village. Development-only until its existing art/audio provenance and legal-distance opinion are complete.
- Premium 1: `Estúdio em Caos`, an original fictional movie-studio comedy. No film, studio, actor, character, costume, quote, logo, clip or music reference.
- Premium 2: `Órbita de Sabotagem`, an original space-opera sabotage world with wholly original factions and visual language.
- Folklore and political/satirical concepts remain blocked until specialist editorial and legal review.

## Go-to-market

Ship the independent PWA first. Measure landing → join/create → completion → rematch → account → offer → purchase. Distribute through WhatsApp invitations, QR sharing, family/party creators, board-game cafés, organic short video and search content. Do not buy broad acquisition before retention is proven.

Internal promotions occupy the post-match placement during beta. An external, contextual, non-personalized provider is considered only after 1,000 monthly active adult hosts and privacy review. The product does not send advertising identifiers for guests, minors or unknown-age players.

## Unit economics and operating guardrails

Initial commercial infrastructure budget: approximately US$45/month for application/database paid tiers and around US$95/month once production realtime-media capacity is required, excluding media transfer and overages. Revalidate vendor pricing before approval; these are planning numbers, not embedded assumptions in code.

Track gross sales, store/payment fees, refunds, taxes, media minutes/GB, support load and net revenue by source. The operating target is media expense below 20% of net revenue. Advertising is a trial subsidy, not the core business.

## Stage gates

1. Remove third-party material, complete provenance and legal/brand clearance.
2. Independent PWA with adult-host controls, starter and internal promotion.
3. Commerce, restoration, deterministic progression and two cleared premium worlds.
4. Organic validation through private invitations and group venues.
5. After a trailing 30-day window reaches 2,000 active adult hosts, 5,000 completed matches, 15% D30 retention, 3% purchaser conversion, 75% completion and media under 20% of net revenue, fund Android and iOS together.
6. Native clients use Capacitor but ship local app UI and native deep links, QR, sharing, haptics, push invitations, restoration and permissions. Store digital purchases use Apple IAP/Google Play Billing through RevenueCat; PWA purchases use Mercado Pago Pix/card.

Missing a gate pauses the next investment stage. It does not trigger urgency mechanics, broader ads or a thin webview submission.
